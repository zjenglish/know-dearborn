5How we defend them

Don't allow a goal in the first ten minutes.
WE DO
  • Fullbacks: slow their wings down. Delay and wait for help.
  • Center back steps: everyone pinches in, weak side winger helps on D.
  • #6 / holding mid: stay in front of the back 4 and win the 2nd ball, over or through. Their #8 plays it in direct, first time.
  • Back line together. #6 covers, fullbacks tuck in.
  • Challenge every ball, on the ground and in the air.

6How we attack them

WE DO
  • Use our wings. Get the ball wide.
  • Targets: be active, check early. Midfield finds the targets and makes runs in behind.
  • Find the high target, set it, play wide or through the half spaces.
  • Weak side winger comes central to help the striker and makes runs in behind.
  • Midfield: weight of pass, follow your pass.
  • Break lines and find feet when we can. When we can't, clear high and wide.
  • Anticipate the run, put the ball into space in behind.
  • Press together. Win territory in their defensive third.
  • Finishing in the box: lay-offs, keep the ball low.
  • Our set pieces: start wide and attack the ball. We can go short on corners.

7Game flow

  • First 10 minStay compact, read their shape, no risky passes in the middle.
  • 10 to 30 minTrap them at the sideline. Test the space behind their wing-backs.
  • Before halfStay locked in. Don't give up a goal off a transition or set piece.
  • 2nd half, tiedPick our moments to press harder. Fresh legs out wide.
  • We're winningMidfield compact, make them play around us, counter when space opens.
  • We're losingWingers more aggressive, press higher.
LINES TOGETHER. EVERY BALL. SPRINT!Keep lines together in transition. Compete for every ball.
